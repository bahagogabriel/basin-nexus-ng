About Basin Nexus NG

Connecting landscapes, resources and knowledge.

Basin Nexus NG is being developed as an accessible geographic knowledge atlas for Nigeria's strategic catchments. Its long-term goal is to connect physical geography with water infrastructure, ecosystem and biodiversity resources, environmental pressures, stakeholder perspectives and development interventions.

Public and evidence-led

Information will be accompanied by clear provenance, dates, geographic precision and verification status. Stakeholder statements are not automatically classified as confirmed facts.

20 catchments, one connected atlas

The current preview uses the 20 catchment polygons supplied for the project. Benue–Mada replaces the historic name Okwa–Mada.

Spatial accuracy

Catchment geometries were checked and repaired; the map uses a simplified display version. Administrative intersections and asset locations are not yet approved.

Respectful publication

Signed documents, attendance registers, private contact details and source statements will not be published without appropriate review and permission.

Current release scope

This release intentionally displays only reviewed catchment names, their supplied geographic outlines, calculated areas and a historical alias. It does not contain the confidential internal stakeholder evidence catalogue or unpublished resource coordinates.

What is not yet in the public catalogue?

Stakeholder-reported resources, infrastructure condition, mineral occurrences, natural heritage areas, proposed interventions, state/LGA boundary intersections and project documents remain in internal QA until source locations and publication status are reviewed.

Methods and source notes

Catchment areas were calculated from the project working geometry in EPSG:6933. For web display, the validated polygons were simplified with topology preservation using a 300 m tolerance in a projected coordinate system, without materially changing their mapped extents. The original shapefile and full-resolution working GeoPackage remain outside the public site.

The Nigeria state and LGA boundary crosswalk is pending independent verification. The proposed external reference is geoBoundaries / GRID3 (CC BY 4.0), subject to dataset QA and attribution on adoption.
