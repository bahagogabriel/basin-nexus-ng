How stakeholder information is attributed

Source: institutional stakeholder engagement for the Strategic Catchment Management Plan (SCMP), attended by representatives of relevant institutions. Information in the communiqués is stakeholder-reported and is not automatically a measured fact, a verified asset location, or the formal endorsement of every participating institution. Individual findings will appear only after privacy, provenance, scientific and publication review. Signed documents, attendance and personal information are excluded.

About Basin Nexus NG

Connecting landscapes, resources and knowledge.

Basin Nexus NG is being developed as an accessible geographic knowledge atlas for Nigeria's strategic catchments. Its long-term goal is to connect physical geography with water infrastructure, ecosystem and biodiversity resources, environmental pressures, stakeholder perspectives and development interventions.

Public and evidence-led

Information will be accompanied by clear provenance, dates, geographic precision and verification status. Stakeholder statements are not automatically classified as confirmed facts.

20 catchments, one connected atlas

The current preview uses the 20 catchment polygons supplied for the project. Benue–Mada replaces the historic name Okwa–Mada.

Spatial accuracy

Catchment geometries were checked and repaired; the map uses a simplified display version. The separate Nigeria boundary is Natural Earth Admin 0 at 1:110m scale (public domain, illustrative only). Catchment boundaries have not been clipped; administrative intersections and asset locations are not yet approved.

Respectful publication

Signed documents, attendance registers, private contact details and source statements will not be published without appropriate review and permission.

Northern Nigeria study footprint

The source study focuses primarily on Northern Nigeria. Catchment boundaries are hydrological, not political: some extend across international borders. The separate dashed Nigeria outline provides geographic context only and is not a substitute for an official surveyed boundary.

Current release scope

The current public atlas brings together all 20 catchment boundaries, provisional state/LGA intersections, national geographic context and modelled FAO AQUASTAT 2026 river reaches for each catchment. Natural Earth provides overview-scale major rivers and waterbodies. These geographic datasets carry source and accuracy notes and remain distinct from confidential stakeholder submissions. The platform does not yet publish unverified dam locations, stakeholder evidence records, signed documents or contact details.

Nigeria political boundary context

The switchable national outline uses Natural Earth Admin 0 Countries at 1:110m (public domain). It is a small-scale visual reference, not a legal/survey boundary. The cross-border classifications are indicative and based on approximate overlays of these generalised lines; do not use them for jurisdictional or area statistics.

Detailed catchment rivers and regional water context

Rivers: selected catchment now loads the 2026 FAO AQUASTAT river reach network within the selected catchment. Use River detail to show main reaches, main and tributaries, or all modeled reaches. Rivers: regional context instead shows coarse but continuous Natural Earth 1:10m major rivers across the catchments. Detailed lines are clipped to the supplied catchment polygons, so source connectivity may cross those displayed boundaries.

Modelled river reaches are based on a 15-arcsecond network, not a complete survey of perennial and seasonal streams. Main/tributary/small labels reflect the source's upstream drainage area thresholds of 1,000 and 100 km²; these are not Strahler orders. FAO AQUASTAT (2026) CC BY 4.0; HydroRIVERS-derived model. Natural Earth overview-scale rivers and lakes are mapped as generalised blue lines and waterbody polygons. This limited overview includes a few major rivers and large waterbodies only. It is not a comprehensive local drainage network, verified dam register, reservoir inventory, or infrastructure location database. The full transboundary catchment area is retained. State and LGA map layers (provisional)

State and LGA counts in individual catchment profiles were calculated by intersecting the simplified catchments with the 2022 GRID3 Nigeria state and LGA polygons distributed through geoBoundaries gbOpen. These are provisional area-based relationships; they are not jurisdictional claims, a list of workshop participants, or current official LGA allocations. Some catchments extend outside Nigeria. The mapped outside-Nigeria fractions use the more detailed ADM1 union and may differ from the 1:110m Natural Earth context. The map has switchable state/FCT boundary lines for all 37 units and an optional layer showing only the relevant intersecting LGA boundaries clipped to the selected catchment. It contains 495 distinct relevant LGA polygons, not all 774 nationally. Administrative geometry is a simplified display derivative; the original intersection statistics were calculated separately.

Stakeholder knowledge index

The public Knowledge index identifies section availability across 20 internal institutional engagement communiqués. Four consistent themes are indexed for every catchment. This release includes no private documents, individual claims or facility coordinates. Download the metadata-only index. Intervention descriptions require additional structured review.

What is not yet in the public catalogue?

Stakeholder-reported resources, infrastructure condition, dam and irrigation locations, mineral occurrences, natural heritage areas, proposed interventions and private project documents remain in internal review until source locations and publication status are verified. Provisional state/LGA geometric intersections and modelled river reaches are already available above, with limitations clearly identified.

Methods and source notes

Catchment areas were calculated from the project working geometry in EPSG:6933. For web display, the validated polygons were simplified with topology preservation using a 300 m tolerance in a projected coordinate system, without materially changing their mapped extents. The original shapefile and full-resolution working GeoPackage remain outside the public site.

The provisional state/LGA intersections have been calculated from geoBoundaries / GRID3 (2022), CC BY 4.0. Administrative source precision, cross-border interpretation and project acceptance remain under review. Only public geometric relationships are included; sensitive stakeholder records remain excluded.
